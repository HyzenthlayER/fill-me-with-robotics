# fill-me-with-robotics

**A blank repo. A wide-open future.**

Right now, this is an empty home for robotics software. What it becomes is anyone's guess. That's the fun of it.

Every PR opens another possibility. The next contribution might change the whole story. A year from now, we hope to be looking at something none of us could have planned.

The code starts at zero. The possibilities don't.

## Your move

Fork the repo, make your contribution, and open a PR. No pitch or advance permission needed. Tell us what changed, how it connects to robotics software, and what you checked.

Choose your own approach. There is no required programming language, framework, or architecture, and no roadmap to follow. The [contribution rules](CONTRIBUTING.md) keep the shared space usable without choosing its future.

## A little structure, plenty of room

Contributions steer the project. Review focuses on concrete problems and the contribution rules; personal taste doesn't decide what belongs here.

Codex is the intended first reviewer once the owner enables automatic review for every PR. Its instructions live in [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), with setup covered by the [official OpenAI documentation](https://learn.chatgpt.com/docs/third-party/github). The owner handles merges with a light touch. Automated review does not automatically merge PRs or certify that software is safe to run.

Changes to the rules happen openly through PRs too. The history is part of the experiment, so keep it intact and explain reversions or declined contributions.

## Contributors

The people who show up will make this place what it is. Meet the [contributors](CONTRIBUTORS.md), or become part of what comes next.

Started **2026-10-23**. Follow the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) and [PR history](https://github.com/fan-ziqi/fill-me-with-robotics/pulls) as the story unfolds.
